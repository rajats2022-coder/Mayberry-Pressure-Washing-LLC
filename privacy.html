Privacy

Privacy and analytics choices

Mayberry Pressure Washing keeps optional measurement separate from the information you choose to send when requesting an estimate.

Estimate requests

The estimate form is processed by Formspree. The information you enter—such as your name, email, phone number, property location, service request, and message—is used to respond to your request. Do not submit sensitive financial, medical, or identity information.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics is disabled until you choose “Allow analytics.” If allowed, it helps measure page visits and actions such as quote, contact, and phone-button clicks. Mayberry does not intentionally send names, email addresses, phone numbers, form contents, or URL query strings to Google Analytics. Advertising storage, advertising personalization, and Google signals are disabled.

Your choice

You can use the site with analytics disabled. Global Privacy Control and browser Do Not Track signals are honored by keeping optional analytics off. Your choice is stored only in this browser.
